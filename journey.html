Looking Back

Looking backward, my path appears fragmented. History. Geology. Biology. Ecology. Data Science. Digital Society. At first glance, these seem unrelated. But over time I realized that the disciplines were changing while the questions remained surprisingly similar.

  • What patterns lie beneath complexity?
  • How do systems organize themselves?
  • Why do similar structures emerge in different contexts?
  • How can we distinguish appearance from mechanism?

The subjects changed. The questions stayed. And this website is, in many ways, a record of that continuing search.
