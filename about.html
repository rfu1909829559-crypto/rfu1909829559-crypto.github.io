My Approach to AI

People often ask whether I'm optimistic or pessimistic about AI. The short answer is neither — I believe AI should remain fundamentally human-centered. That view comes down to three ideas that shape how I think about AI throughout this site:

From Human

AI begins with human creativity, intention, and purpose. Current systems can assist, generate, and recombine ideas, but meaningful direction still comes from people. I view AI as a creative tool rather than an independent originator.

With Human

Powerful systems require meaningful oversight. As AI becomes more capable, accountability becomes more important, not less. Human judgment should remain part of decision-making, evaluation, and responsibility.

To Human

Technology should ultimately serve human flourishing. The purpose of AI is not simply to automate tasks, but to expand human capability, support human well-being, and help solve meaningful problems.
